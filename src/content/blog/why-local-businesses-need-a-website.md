---
title: Why every local business needs a website
date: 2026-10-31
description: A Google listing is not enough. Here is what a real website adds.
---
Customers search on their phones before they visit or call. A website gives them your services, prices, photos, location and a way to contact you, all in one place that **you** control.

A good local business website should:

- Load fast on mobile
- Show your phone number and WhatsApp button clearly
- Include a map and opening hours
- Collect inquiries or bookings with a simple form
- Be set up for Google so people can find you

If you do not have a website yet, even a simple one-page site is a strong start.
